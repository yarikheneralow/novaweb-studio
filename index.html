05 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Still wondering about your project? We're happy to talk through the details.

How long does a typical website take? +

A basic landing page may take around 1–2 weeks after we receive your content and feedback. A larger website needs more time. The actual timeline is confirmed before work starts.

Can you update my existing website? +

Yes. We can review your current setup and propose improvements, redesigns, or maintenance work.

Will I own my website? +

Ownership, hosting, licenses, and access are agreed upon before the project begins. We aim for transparent handovers.

Can AI be added later? +

Often, yes. We can start with a website and explore AI assistants or automations when there is a clear business use case.
